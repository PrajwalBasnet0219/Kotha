2. BLUEPRINT EDITOR - FOCUS AREA (replace market/catalogue emphasis)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
The product sells the BLUEPRINT and a PREVIEW of how the room/building/house looks.
It does NOT sell furniture. The catalogue is a reference aid only.

Editor must support:
  - Blank sheet or upload a photo of an existing plan
  - 100 mm grid with 15 degree rotation snapping
  - Two-point scale calibration (one real distance -> whole sheet in mm)
  - Wall runs: draw, extend, trim, join (min length enforced)
  - Doors: place and slide along a wall; swing direction
  - Windows: place and slide along a wall; sill height
  - Room labels + auto area / perimeter
  - Dimensions: show on demand, never hidden behind hover
  - Up to 3 rooms joined by doorways on shared walls
  - Undo / redo, layers (walls, openings, furniture, dimensions)
  - Export: PDF + SVG plan, furniture schedule CSV, version snapshot

Accuracy rules (a blueprint must be buildable):
  - Walls must form closed rooms before a room is created
  - Openings (door/window) must sit on a wall and not overlap another opening
  - Door/window width must fit the wall with clearance at both ends
  - Wall thickness and height are set once and applied everywhere
  - Dimensions are always in millimetres, never rounded for display
  - A plan that does not close cannot generate a 3D room (show why)

Object placement validity (no invalid objects outside the box):
  - A piece of furniture must be placed inside a room boundary
  - A piece cannot straddle two rooms unless explicitly joined
  - Clearance rules are enforced (e.g. 600 mm in front of a door)
  - A piece that does not fit is never offered as a valid placement
  - Show the reason a piece is invalid (too wide, blocks a door, outside room)
  - Example: kitchen with 2 rooms -> a sofa placed outside those rooms is invalid

9. BLUEPRINT SUMMARY PANEL (top right of the sheet)
--------------------------------------------------
  - Always visible summary card anchored to the top right of the blueprint
  - Lists the construction materials needed to build what is drawn:
      * Cement (bags), sand (m3), aggregate (m3), steel (kg)
      * Bricks/blocks (count), timber (m), plywood (sheets)
      * Paint (litres), primer, waterproofing
      * Doors and windows (count by size)
      * Plumbing/electrical points (count)
  - Quantities are estimated from the traced walls, floors, and openings
  - Recalculates live as the plan changes
  - Exported with the plan (PDF/SVG) and as a material schedule CSV
  - Clearly marked as an estimate, not a quote

10. MULTI-FLOOR BLUEPRINTS
--------------------------
  - A project can have 1 or many floors (ground, 1st, 2nd, ...)
  - Each floor is its own plan sheet with its own walls, openings, rooms
  - Floors share a common grid and scale (calibrated once per project)
  - Staircase/void openings link floors where they meet
  - Add, remove, or reorder floors at any time
  - A floor can be edited later to add rooms, walls, or openings
  - 3D room/building preview shows all floors stacked
  - Export per floor or the whole building
  - Floor count and labels appear in the project header and the summary panel
